# VALORANT Champions Tour — Official Rulebook (Simplified Extract)

## 1. Tournament Format

### 1.1 Group Stage
- Teams are divided into groups of four (4).
- Each group plays a Double-Elimination bracket (GSL format).
- All matches in the Group Stage are Best-of-3 (Bo3).
- The top two (2) teams from each group advance to the Playoff Stage.

### 1.2 Playoff Stage
- The Playoff Stage uses a Single-Elimination bracket.
- Quarterfinals and Semifinals are Best-of-3 (Bo3).
- The Grand Final is Best-of-5 (Bo5).

### 1.3 Map Pool
The active competitive map pool consists of seven (7) maps:
1. Abyss
2. Ascent
3. Bind
4. Haven
5. Lotus
6. Pearl
7. Split

### 1.4 Map Veto Process
1. Team A bans one map.
2. Team B bans one map.
3. Team A picks one map.
4. Team B picks one map.
5. Team A bans one map.
6. Team B bans one map.
7. The remaining map is the decider (if needed).

## 2. Match Rules

### 2.1 Match Start
- Teams must have a minimum of five (5) players connected and ready.
- Players have a fifteen (15) minute grace period from the scheduled start time.
- Failure to have five (5) players ready results in a default loss.

### 2.2 Side Selection
- The team that did NOT pick the map chooses the starting side (Attack or Defense).
- On the decider map, side selection is determined by a coin toss.

### 2.3 Overtime Rules
- If a map is tied 12-12, Overtime begins.
- Overtime follows the standard Riot-implemented overtime format.
- Teams switch sides every two (2) rounds in overtime.

## 3. Pause Rules

### 3.1 Tactical Pauses
- Each team is allowed two (2) tactical pauses per map.
- Each tactical pause has a maximum duration of sixty (60) seconds.
- Tactical pauses can only be initiated during the buy phase.

### 3.2 Technical Pauses
- Technical pauses can be called at any time if a player experiences a legitimate technical issue.
- The affected player must immediately notify the referee.
- A technical pause has a maximum duration of fifteen (15) minutes.
- If the issue is not resolved within 15 minutes, the team must continue with a substitute or play 4v5.

## 4. Disconnection Rules

### 4.1 Player Disconnection
- If a player disconnects during a round, that round is played out normally (no replay).
- The team has up to ten (10) minutes to reconnect the player.
- If the player cannot reconnect within 10 minutes, the team must:
  - Substitute the player with an eligible substitute, OR
  - Continue the match with fewer than five (5) players.

### 4.2 Server Crash
- If the game server crashes, the match is restarted from the exact round and score.
- All agent selections and loadouts are restored.

## 5. Player Conduct

### 5.1 Communication
- Players must communicate exclusively through in-game voice chat and the designated team communication channel.
- External communication with coaches is prohibited during live rounds.

### 5.2 Coaching
- Coaches may communicate with players only during tactical pauses, technical pauses, and halftime.
- Coaches are NOT allowed to communicate during live gameplay or buy phases.

### 5.3 Prohibited Actions
- Use of any unauthorized third-party software (cheats, exploits).
- Intentional abuse of in-game bugs or exploits.
- Unsportsmanlike conduct, including toxic behavior in all-chat.
- Match fixing or collusion with opposing teams.

## 6. Penalties

| Violation | First Offense | Second Offense | Third Offense |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late to match | Warning | Round forfeit (1 round) | Map forfeit |
| Unauthorized pause | Warning | Round forfeit (1 round) | Round forfeit (3 rounds) |
| Bug exploit | Round forfeit (1-3 rounds) | Map forfeit | Disqualification |
| Toxic behavior | Warning | Fine + suspension | Disqualification |
| Cheating | Immediate disqualification and ban | — | — |
