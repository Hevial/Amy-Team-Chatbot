# VALORANT Patch Notes — Episode 9 Act 2 (Patch 9.04)

## Release Date: August 5, 2026

---

## Agent Updates

### Jett
- **Tailwind (E):**
  - Dash distance reduced from 7.5m to 6.5m.
  - Activation window after pressing ability reduced from 12s to 10s.
- **Cloudburst (C):**
  - Smoke duration reduced from 4.5s to 3.5s.
  - Cost increased from 200 to 250 credits.

### Omen
- **Paranoia (Q):**
  - Projectile speed increased from 2800 to 3200.
  - Nearsight duration remains 2.5s.
- **Dark Cover (E):**
  - Smoke duration increased from 15s to 17.5s.
  - Recharge time reduced from 40s to 35s.
- **From the Shadows (X):**
  - Ultimate cost reduced from 7 to 6 points.
  - Now shows a brief indicator on the minimap when Omen begins teleporting (visible to enemies for 0.5s).

### Cypher
- **Trapwire (C):**
  - Concuss duration on triggered enemies increased from 3s to 3.5s.
  - Health increased from 20 to 30 HP.
- **Spycam (E):**
  - Dart tracking duration increased from 4s to 5s.
  - Camera can now be recalled 1s faster (from 15s to 14s cooldown).

### Raze
- **Boom Bot (C):**
  - Explosion damage reduced from 80 to 65.
  - Lock-on range reduced from 15m to 12m.
- **Showstopper (X):**
  - Rocket travel speed increased by 15%.
  - Explosion radius remains 6m.
  - Damage at the center remains 150 (lethal at full armor).

### Deadlock
- **Barrier Mesh (E):**
  - Wall health per segment increased from 400 to 500 HP.
  - Deployment time reduced from 1.2s to 0.9s.
- **Annihilation (X):**
  - Cocoon pull speed increased by 20%.
  - Ultimate cost reduced from 8 to 7 points.

---

## Weapon Updates

### Outlaw
- **Price:** Reduced from 2,400 to 2,250 credits.
- **Fire rate:** Increased from 0.75 to 0.85 rounds/second.
- **Leg damage multiplier:** Increased from 0.75x to 0.85x.

### Stinger
- **Price:** Reduced from 1,100 to 950 credits.
- **First bullet accuracy:** Improved by 10%.
- **Full spray recoil:** Slightly reduced horizontal spread.

---

## Map Updates

### Split
- **A Site:**
  - Widened A Main entrance by 0.5m.
  - Removed one of the two boxes on A Ramps.
- **Mid:**
  - Added a new small cover box near Mid Vent entrance.
  - Mail box area slightly rearranged for better attacker approach.

### Pearl
- **B Site:**
  - B Long wall extended to provide additional cover for defenders rotating from Mid.
  - Screen on B Site repositioned 1m to the right.

---

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed an issue where Killjoy's Turret could be placed inside certain walls on Lotus.
- Fixed Sova's Recon Bolt not revealing enemies behind Deadlock's Barrier Mesh.
- Fixed a rare crash when Omen used From the Shadows during round transition.
- Fixed Cypher's Spycam showing incorrect camera angle after being recalled on Ascent.
- Fixed inconsistent weapon skin animations when switching between Classic and Sidearm.

---

## Quality of Life
- Added a new "Agent Stat Tracker" widget to the Career tab.
- Improved the visibility of ability cooldown timers in the HUD.
- Deathmatch now supports 12 players (up from 10).
- Added a "Replay Last Round" button in custom games for practice purposes.
