# Istanbul Invitational 2026 — Tournament Format & Schedule

- **Event:** Istanbul Invitational 2026
- **Game:** VALORANT
- **Date:** September 15–22, 2026
- **Location:** Istanbul Expo Center, Istanbul, Turkey
- **Teams:** 16 invited teams
- **Total Prize Pool:** $200,000 USD

## Group Stage Format

### Group Assignments
Teams are seeded into four (4) groups of four (4) teams each based on regional rankings.

- **Group A:** Team Alpha, Team Beta, Team Gamma, Amnesia Esports
- **Group B:** Team Delta, Team Epsilon, Team Zeta, Team Eta
- **Group C:** Team Theta, Team Iota, Team Kappa, Team Lambda
- **Group D:** Team Mu, Team Nu, Team Xi, Team Omicron

### Bracket Format
Each group uses a **Double-Elimination GSL bracket**:
1. **Opening Match:** Seed 1 vs Seed 4, Seed 2 vs Seed 3
2. **Winners Match:** Winners of Opening Matches play each other (winner advances to Playoffs)
3. **Elimination Match:** Losers of Opening Matches play each other (loser is eliminated)
4. **Decider Match:** Loser of Winners Match vs Winner of Elimination Match (winner advances, loser eliminated)

All Group Stage matches are **Best-of-3 (Bo3)**.

## Playoff Stage

### Format
- **Single-Elimination bracket** with 8 teams (top 2 from each group).
- Quarterfinals: Best-of-3 (Bo3)
- Semifinals: Best-of-3 (Bo3)
- Grand Final: Best-of-5 (Bo5)

## Travel & Logistics

### Team Requirements
- Each team must provide a roster of exactly **five (5) players and one (1) coach**.
- Up to two (2) substitute players may be registered.
- All roster changes must be submitted at least 72 hours before the event.

### Venue Information
- **Practice rooms** available from September 13 (2 days before the event).
- Each team is assigned a 2-hour practice slot per day.
- Tournament PCs are provided with standard peripherals (players may bring their own mouse and keyboard).
- Internet connection: dedicated 1 Gbps line for the tournament server.

### Accommodation
- Hotel provided for all 16 teams (double occupancy rooms).
- Breakfast and lunch included at the venue.
- Shuttle service between hotel and venue from 08:00 to 23:00 daily.
